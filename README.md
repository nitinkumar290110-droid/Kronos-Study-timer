# ⏳ Kronos - The Zero-Fluff Study Stopwatch

**Kronos** is an ultra-minimal, distraction-free stopwatch-style timer designed specifically for students and professionals to record their daily study sessions and focus blocks. 

Built in just one hour using **Gemini AI**, Kronos eliminates all the bloat, mandatory accounts, and aggressive monetization found in modern productivity apps. Open it, hit start, and focus.

---

## 🚀 Features

* **Zero Friction:** No signup, no email verification, and no onboarding screens. 
* **Distraction-Free:** Strictly no pop-up ads, flashing banners, or tracking scripts to interrupt your workflow.
* **Ultra-Lightweight:** Instantly loads with a simple 2-to-3 button interface.
* **Built in Public (AI Experiment):** Coded entirely via AI prompting as a productive weekend project.

---

## 📲 How to Install

1. Download the latest `Kronos_Study_Timer.apk` file from this repository.
2. Open the file on your Android device.
3. If prompted, allow your browser or file manager to "Install unknown apps" (this is standard for downloading apps outside the Google Play Store).
4. Launch Kronos and start tracking your study sessions!

## 🧠 The Story Behind Kronos

As a student grinding through exams, I grew incredibly frustrated with over-complicated timer apps that required subscription tiers just to count minutes. I didn't have the time to code a massive app from scratch, so I spent an hour collaborating with Gemini AI to generate exactly what I needed. 

Kronos is named after the Greek god/personification of time. It's meant to help you master your schedule, not steal your data.
